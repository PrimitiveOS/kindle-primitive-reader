RTF DOS code-page mapping data

Reader's RTF decoder uses tables generated from Unicode's published
Microsoft CP437 and CP850 mapping files, dated 24 April 1996:
https://www.unicode.org/Public/MAPPINGS/VENDORS/MICSFT/PC/CP437.TXT
https://www.unicode.org/Public/MAPPINGS/VENDORS/MICSFT/PC/CP850.TXT
