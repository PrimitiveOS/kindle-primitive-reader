The unmodified upstream TeX file is included in hyph-no.tex. Reader compiles
its supported single-word patterns and selected exceptions into native lookup
tables corresponding to pinned KOReader rules after case normalization. One
source pattern and 18 unhyphenated exceptions are excluded; 21 uppercase
hyphenated exceptions are retained lowercased as in KOReader. The tables
are a derived form, not the original TeX file.
