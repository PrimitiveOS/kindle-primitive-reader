# Page Browser thumbnail numbers

The Page Browser **Grid** panel offers three **Thumbnail page numbers** modes: **None**, **Row start**, and **All**. Row start labels the first thumbnail in each visual row; All is the existing default. Thumbnail touch targets, the selected-page indicator, bookmark icons, and hidden-page indicators remain available in every mode. The original reading position is unchanged until a thumbnail is opened.

Changing the mode saves a choice for the current book. **Save number default** saves the displayed choice for books without their own choice. **Use default numbers** removes the current book's override, so it follows the Reader default again. Book settings are part of the library and follow its existing save, backup, merge, and rollback paths. The default is stored with Reader controls and follows the existing reading-backup behavior for those controls. Older data without these fields continues to show all page numbers.

KOReader's pinned [page-number modes](https://github.com/koreader/koreader/blob/9192014d8bd82a91dc1012473be0f238dedfdb54/frontend/ui/widget/pagebrowserwidget.lua#L331-L366) and [Page Browser menu](https://github.com/koreader/koreader/blob/9192014d8bd82a91dc1012473be0f238dedfdb54/frontend/ui/widget/pagebrowserwidget.lua#L1080-L1104) provide the reference. Reader presents labels inside its existing thumbnail controls instead of KOReader's side labels. This implementation still requires simulation, accessibility, and Kindle display and persistence validation.

For fixed-layout documents, the scrub ribbon displays ticks at stable chapter destinations and shows the latest section title at or before the browsed page. Reader-authored custom contents take precedence when enabled; otherwise the fixed document's stable publisher outline is used. For reflow documents, the track instead represents spine chapters: ticks identify chapters containing entries in the active custom, alternative or publisher Contents, and the caption shows the browsed spine chapter's label. When no publisher Contents exists, chapter entries provide the ticks. Entries within the same chapter are coalesced, so a tick does not identify the exact page or location of a heading. Unresolved destinations are omitted. Marks are quantized to the available ribbon width, invalid destinations are ignored, and mirrored layouts reverse their positions. Scrubbing, thumbnail actions, and reading position are unchanged. KOReader's [chapter spans in the bottom ribbon](https://github.com/koreader/koreader/blob/9192014d8bd82a91dc1012473be0f238dedfdb54/frontend/ui/widget/pagebrowserwidget.lua#L580-L630) inspired this bounded view. Exact within-chapter spans and the full multilayer layout remain open. This addition has not yet been tested in simulation or on Kindle.

Grid's **Chapter levels** control chooses how many outline depths contribute chapter ticks. Zero hides them; the maximum is the deepest available level, capped at 16. Publisher/alternative and custom Contents choices are saved separately for each book, and old books without a choice show all available levels. A saved choice is clamped for display when the current Contents has fewer levels; it is not overwritten until changed. Fixed section captions follow the chosen depth; the reflow caption remains the spine chapter label. This filters Reader's current chapter-level ribbon rather than creating KOReader's multilayer spans. KOReader's [depth control](https://github.com/koreader/koreader/blob/9192014d8bd82a91dc1012473be0f238dedfdb54/frontend/ui/widget/pagebrowserwidget.lua#L1107-L1130) and [per-document settings](https://github.com/koreader/koreader/blob/9192014d8bd82a91dc1012473be0f238dedfdb54/frontend/ui/widget/pagebrowserwidget.lua#L1248-L1258) provide the reference. Layout, persistence and Kindle behavior still need validation.

The ribbon's handle identifies the browsed location. A separate upward caret identifies the actual reading page, or the reading spine chapter in a reflow book. Filled and hollow squares mark up to three recent previous and next navigation locations respectively. Fixed documents use page positions; reflow books resolve a history anchor's publication and chapter path to a spine chapter, or use a bounded saved chapter for a legacy anchorless position. Raw anchors use the background verification described below; unresolvable anchors are omitted. The reflow cue is chapter-level only: it does not resolve source revision or distinguish locations within a chapter. Nearby positions can be grouped, and the reading caret takes visual precedence when markers overlap. The Grid panel shows the symbol key. Invalid history positions are omitted. These marks do not change navigation, stored history or the reading position. KOReader's [ribbon description](https://github.com/koreader/koreader/blob/9192014d8bd82a91dc1012473be0f238dedfdb54/frontend/ui/widget/pagebrowserwidget.lua#L1145-L1163) motivated these cues; numbered individual and precise reflow page markers remain open. The markers have not yet been validated in simulation or on Kindle.

On the ribbon, short gray marks indicate highlights, short black marks indicate highlights with notes, and taller black marks indicate page bookmarks. Fixed documents include all pages of a multi-page highlight. Reflow documents indicate only the spine chapter containing each bookmark or highlight's start: an anchor must identify the current publication and a known chapter; raw anchors additionally require source verification, while legacy anchorless bookmarks use their bounded saved chapter. These chapter-level cues do not verify source revisions, identify exact pages, or show cross-chapter continuation. Nearby positions are coalesced into screen-width buckets; overlapping marks and the browsing handle can hide individual cues. Invalid positions are ignored. These are visual indicators only and do not change track hit targets or reading position. Exact reflow page markers remain open. The indicators still require simulation and Kindle validation.

Raw-anchor ribbon cues are progressively verified using a cancellable background job rather than opening chapters while the screen is drawn. The current and already-previewed chapter probes can resolve raw bookmarks and history immediately; other chapters are checked in the worker. Raw highlights appear after both endpoints have been source-verified and their ordering checked. Each chapter layout is isolated and checked against the captured book and font sources. The worker scans at most 128 entries and handles at most four jobs per slice; verified ribbon positions are coalesced into 256 bins. The result cache holds at most six history anchors and four thumbnail quotes. Results are discarded when the reader closes the overview, sleeps, resumes from storage, or detects a changed layout/source snapshot. Cues that fail verification or are still pending are omitted. A later completion repaints the overview without moving the book or changing marks.

Thumbnails show a highlight icon on pages containing a saved highlight, or a note/edit icon when any highlight on the page also has a nonblank note. Fixed documents include continuation pages of multi-page selections. Reflow documents use the existing current or preview chapter layout and mark pages where a verified selection produces visible geometry; unresolvable anchors are skipped. To bound work on heavily annotated books, at most 512 reflow page/selection geometry checks are performed per draw, so later marks may be omitted from that thumbnail window. The existing bookmark indicator remains in the opposite corner, and thumbnail tap and hold actions are unchanged. This Reader-specific thumbnail cue was inspired by KOReader's highlight and note indicators on the page ribbon. Exact KOReader ribbon symbols and cross-chapter reflow highlights remain open, and the new cues still need simulation and Kindle validation.

For a raw highlight crossing chapter boundaries, the worker also verifies the complete saved quote before the current thumbnail chapter may display a local fragment. The thumbnail view retains its existing limit of four cross-chapter checks per draw and a maximum span of eight chapters; failed, pending, or over-limit selections remain omitted. The worker checks cancellation between chapter layouts, enforces the saved-highlight text limit, and preserves ODT continuation separators. These changes need simulation and device validation.

The Grid panel's **Help** button explains the implemented thumbnail, page-button, edge-swipe and ribbon controls, visual cues, and settings. Back returns to Grid without changing the book or browser position. This follows the intent of KOReader's [Page Browser About and gestures help](https://github.com/koreader/koreader/blob/9192014d8bd82a91dc1012473be0f238dedfdb54/frontend/ui/widget/pagebrowserwidget.lua#L1145-L1207); Reader's text describes its own behavior. The layout and translations still require simulation and Kindle validation.

The visible **Return to book** button closes the Page Browser and any nested Book Map/Page Browser stack, returning to the existing reading location rather than opening the browsed thumbnail. **Back** retains its normal one-level navigation. KOReader supports [closing parent browsers together](https://github.com/koreader/koreader/blob/9192014d8bd82a91dc1012473be0f238dedfdb54/frontend/ui/widget/pagebrowserwidget.lua#L1209-L1220); Reader exposes a separate touch control for this action. The new control still requires simulation and Kindle validation.
