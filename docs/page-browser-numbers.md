# Page Browser thumbnail numbers

The Page Browser **Grid** panel offers three **Thumbnail page numbers** modes: **None**, **Row start**, and **All**. Row start labels the first thumbnail in each visual row; All is the existing default. Thumbnail touch targets, the selected-page indicator, bookmark icons, and hidden-page indicators remain available in every mode. The original reading position is unchanged until a thumbnail is opened.

Changing the mode saves a choice for the current book. **Set as default** saves the displayed choice for books without their own choice. **Use default** removes the current book's override, so it follows the Reader default again. Book settings are part of the library and follow its existing save, backup, merge, and rollback paths. The default is stored with Reader controls and follows the existing reading-backup behavior for those controls. Older data without these fields continues to show all page numbers.

KOReader's pinned [page-number modes](https://github.com/koreader/koreader/blob/9192014d8bd82a91dc1012473be0f238dedfdb54/frontend/ui/widget/pagebrowserwidget.lua#L331-L366) and [Page Browser menu](https://github.com/koreader/koreader/blob/9192014d8bd82a91dc1012473be0f238dedfdb54/frontend/ui/widget/pagebrowserwidget.lua#L1080-L1104) provide the reference. Reader presents labels inside its existing thumbnail controls instead of KOReader's side labels. This implementation still requires simulation, accessibility, and Kindle display and persistence validation.

For fixed-layout documents, the scrub ribbon displays ticks at stable chapter destinations and shows the latest section title at or before the browsed page. Reader-authored custom contents take precedence when enabled; otherwise the fixed document's stable publisher outline is used. For reflow documents, the track instead represents spine chapters: ticks identify chapters containing entries in the active custom, alternative or publisher Contents, and the caption shows the browsed spine chapter's label. When no publisher Contents exists, chapter entries provide the ticks. Entries within the same chapter are coalesced, so a tick does not identify the exact page or location of a heading. Unresolved destinations are omitted. Marks are quantized to the available ribbon width, invalid destinations are ignored, and mirrored layouts reverse their positions. Scrubbing, thumbnail actions, and reading position are unchanged. KOReader's [chapter spans in the bottom ribbon](https://github.com/koreader/koreader/blob/9192014d8bd82a91dc1012473be0f238dedfdb54/frontend/ui/widget/pagebrowserwidget.lua#L580-L630) inspired this bounded view. Exact within-chapter spans, adjustable outline depth, and the full multilayer layout remain open. This addition has not yet been tested in simulation or on Kindle.

The ribbon's handle identifies the browsed location. A separate upward caret identifies the actual reading page, or the reading spine chapter in a reflow book. On fixed-layout books, filled and hollow squares mark up to three recent previous and next navigation locations respectively; nearby positions can be grouped, and the reading caret takes visual precedence when markers overlap. The Grid panel shows the symbol key. Invalid history pages are omitted. These marks do not change navigation, stored history or the reading position. KOReader's [ribbon description](https://github.com/koreader/koreader/blob/9192014d8bd82a91dc1012473be0f238dedfdb54/frontend/ui/widget/pagebrowserwidget.lua#L1145-L1163) motivated these cues; numbered individual markers and reflow history locations remain open. The markers have not yet been validated in simulation or on Kindle.

On fixed-document ribbons, short gray marks indicate highlights, short black marks indicate highlights with notes, and taller black marks indicate page bookmarks. All pages of a multi-page highlight are included. Nearby pages are coalesced into screen-width buckets; overlapping marks and the browsing handle can hide individual cues. Invalid positions are ignored. These are visual indicators only and do not change track hit targets or reading position. Reflow saved-mark ribbon positions remain open because its track currently represents whole chapters instead of pages. The indicators still require simulation and Kindle validation.

Thumbnails show a highlight icon on pages containing a saved highlight, or a note/edit icon when any highlight on the page also has a nonblank note. Fixed documents include continuation pages of multi-page selections. Reflow documents use the existing current or preview chapter layout and mark only pages where a same-chapter selection produces visible geometry; raw, cross-chapter or unresolvable anchors are skipped. To bound work on heavily annotated books, at most 512 reflow page/selection geometry checks are performed per draw, so later marks may be omitted from that thumbnail window. The existing bookmark indicator remains in the opposite corner, and thumbnail tap and hold actions are unchanged. This Reader-specific thumbnail cue was inspired by KOReader's highlight and note indicators on the page ribbon. Exact KOReader ribbon symbols and cross-chapter reflow highlights remain open, and the new cues still need simulation and Kindle validation.

The Grid panel's **Help** button explains the implemented thumbnail, page-button, edge-swipe and ribbon controls, visual cues, and settings. Back returns to Grid without changing the book or browser position. This follows the intent of KOReader's [Page Browser About and gestures help](https://github.com/koreader/koreader/blob/9192014d8bd82a91dc1012473be0f238dedfdb54/frontend/ui/widget/pagebrowserwidget.lua#L1145-L1207); Reader's text describes its own behavior. The layout and translations still require simulation and Kindle validation.
